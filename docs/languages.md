# Supported languages

**On your computer (10):** English · Korean · Chinese · Japanese · French · German ·
Italian · Portuguese · Russian · Spanish

**With the Perso API (100+):** the languages follow the voice model picked under
**Dubbing model**: Nightingale has 103, Oriole (the default) 77, Wren 32.

**All three models (32):** Arabic · Bulgarian · Chinese · Croatian · Czech · Danish ·
Dutch · English (US) · Filipino · Finnish · French · German · Greek · Hindi ·
Hungarian · Indonesian · Italian · Japanese · Korean · Malay · Norwegian · Polish ·
Portuguese (Brazil) · Romanian · Russian · Slovak · Spanish (Mexico) · Swedish · Tamil ·
Turkish · Ukrainian · Vietnamese

**Nightingale and Oriole (37):** Afrikaans · Armenian · Assamese · Azerbaijani ·
Belarusian · Bosnian · Catalan · Cebuano · English (UK) · Estonian · Galician ·
Georgian · Gujarati · Hausa · Hebrew · Icelandic · Javanese · Kannada · Kazakh ·
Kyrgyz · Latvian · Lithuanian · Macedonian · Malayalam · Marathi · Nepali · Persian ·
Portuguese (Portugal) · Punjabi · Sindhi · Slovenian · Spanish (Spain) · Swahili ·
Telugu · Thai · Urdu · Welsh

**Nightingale only (34):** Akan · Albanian · Amharic · Arabic (Egypt) · Basque ·
Burmese · Cantonese · Chinese (Taiwan) · Dogri · English (Australia) ·
English (Canada) · French (Canada) · Kikuyu · Kinyarwanda · Kirundi · Luganda ·
Mongolian · Sepedi · Sesotho · Spanish (Argentina) · Spanish (Chile) · Sundanese ·
Swati · Tajik · Tibetan · Tsonga · Tswana · Uyghur · Uzbek · Venda · Waray · Wolof ·
Yoruba · Zulu

**Oriole only (8):** Bengali · Chichewa · Irish · Lingala · Luxembourgish · Pashto ·
Serbian · Somali

The cloud lists are Perso's, as of 2026-10-02 in the copy that ships with the app; while a Perso key is saved, the app asks Perso for the current list, at most once a day. Set **Dubbing** to **Perso API** under Advanced options to use them; it needs a Perso key, and how to add one is in [Configuration](configuration.md).
